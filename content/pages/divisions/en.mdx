## Lumenary Scientia: the sciences, always free and open source

Lumenary Scientia is Lumenary Academy's school of the sciences. Everything in it is, and will always be, free and open source: the code under Apache-2.0 and the lessons, exercises and diagrams under CC BY-SA 4.0. There are no accounts, no advertising and no paid tiers. Anyone may use, translate, adapt and share it.

Mathematics was the first subject: its curriculum runs from counting toward the first year of graduate school, and every topic on the curriculum map has a first-draft lesson. Computer science is the second: Lumenary CS runs from a first program, through algorithms, computer systems and software at scale, to machine learning and how today's large AI models are built, with code that runs in the browser and is tested by the site's own checks. Beside it are courses in the world's major programming languages, from Assembly to Zig, and electives that go deep into one field, starting with blockchains and verifiable computation. Electrical engineering and robotics follow, each from a first circuit or a first moving robot to the frontier of 2026: chips, power grids and radios; motion, control, perception, planning and robot learning. Chemistry runs the same way, from matter and change to AI-assisted discovery and sustainable chemistry. Every lesson in them was written with AI assistance and is still awaiting review by a human expert (see [About](../about/)). The other subjects will follow, built the same way and under the same licences.

## Lumenary Lingua: languages

Lumenary Lingua, at [lingua.lumenary.academy](https://lingua.lumenary.academy), teaches languages, each one through a language the learner already knows: English taught in English, French taught in English, English taught in Spanish, and so on. The first two courses are open, each in six levels from A1 to C2: [Spanish, taught in English](https://lingua.lumenary.academy/en/spanish/) and [English, taught in Spanish](https://lingua.lumenary.academy/es/ingles/). Like Scientia, they are free and need no account, and every lesson was drafted with AI assistance and awaits review by a human teacher. ## Lumenary Prep: exam preparation (paid)

Lumenary Prep, at [prep.lumenary.academy](https://prep.lumenary.academy), prepares learners for exams: college admissions (the SAT, ACT and CLT), graduate and professional school (the GRE General Test and Subject Tests, GMAT and LSAT), English-language tests (IELTS and TOEFL iBT), and professional licences (the Enrolled Agent exam, the U.S. Tax Court nonattorney exam and the CPA Exam).

Lumenary Prep is a paid service. Each course has one price, paid once, for lifetime access. Its facts come from each exam's official sources, and its lessons and practice questions are original writing. Its courses are labelled as AI-drafted until a qualified person has reviewed them, and it makes no score guarantees or pass-rate claims.

Lumenary Prep is separate from Lumenary Scientia. Paying for Prep is never needed to use Scientia, and Scientia stays free and open source.
